## **1\. About this policy**

This Cookies and Similar Technologies Policy explains how MoveLoop uses cookies and similar technologies when you use the MoveLoop website and application.

We use these technologies primarily to provide essential functionality, maintain secure authentication and support specific workflows.

**We do not currently use cookies or similar technologies for advertising, behavioural tracking or marketing analytics.**

This policy should be read alongside our Privacy Policy.

## **2\. What are cookies and similar technologies?**

Cookies are small files stored on your device by a website.

Similar technologies can include browser storage mechanisms such as `localStorage` and `sessionStorage`. These can allow a website to temporarily retain information within your browser to support a particular function or workflow.

MoveLoop uses both cookies and limited browser storage for operational purposes.

## **3\. Cookies we use**

### **Authentication cookies**

MoveLoop uses authentication cookies provided through our authentication infrastructure to maintain your signed-in session.

These cookies allow MoveLoop to:

* recognise an authenticated user;  
* maintain a secure session between pages and requests;  
* support authentication and account security; and  
* maintain your session when using the MoveLoop application.

These are **strictly necessary** for the operation of the authenticated MoveLoop service.

MoveLoop does not use these cookies for advertising or behavioural tracking.

## **4\. Browser storage used for functionality**

MoveLoop may use browser storage to support specific workflows.

### **Invitation workflows**

A temporary browser storage item may retain an invitation token associated with an invitation workflow.

This allows the application to maintain the relevant workflow between pages or steps.

It is cleared when the relevant workflow is completed or otherwise removed.

### **Legal acceptance**

MoveLoop may temporarily store information relating to pending acceptance of the applicable terms and privacy information while an authentication session is being established.

This allows the acceptance to be associated with the correct authenticated account.

The information is removed once the acceptance has been successfully recorded.

### **Estate-agent signup**

During certain estate-agent signup and email-confirmation flows, MoveLoop may temporarily store the contact information required to complete the signup.

This information is subject to a limited seven-day validity period and is cleared once the information has been successfully transferred to the MoveLoop account.

## **5\. Analytics and marketing**

**MoveLoop does not currently use Production cookies or similar technologies for:**

* advertising;  
* behavioural advertising;  
* cross-site tracking;  
* marketing pixels;  
* Google Analytics or equivalent analytics platforms; or  
* third-party advertising networks.

We therefore do not currently use a cookie or consent-management mechanism for those purposes.

If this changes, we will update this policy and implement any consent or other controls required by applicable law before introducing non-essential technologies where required.

## **6\. Third-party services**

MoveLoop uses a number of third-party services to operate the platform.

Some of these services operate entirely through server-to-server communication and therefore do not place cookies on your browser through the MoveLoop application.

For example, address lookup, email delivery and certain infrastructure services are accessed by MoveLoop's servers rather than through browser-based tracking technologies.

Where MoveLoop redirects you to a third-party website or service, that provider may use its own cookies or similar technologies. Those technologies are governed by the relevant provider's policies. 

## **7\. Managing cookies and browser storage**

You can generally control or remove cookies and browser storage through your browser settings.

Please note that removing or blocking strictly necessary cookies or browser storage may prevent some parts of MoveLoop from functioning correctly, including authentication and certain workflows.

Because MoveLoop does not currently use non-essential analytics or advertising cookies, there are no such optional tracking cookies to switch off within the MoveLoop application.

## **8\. Changes to this policy**

We may update this policy when our use of cookies or similar technologies changes, or where necessary to reflect changes to our services or applicable requirements.

The latest version will be made available through the MoveLoop website.
