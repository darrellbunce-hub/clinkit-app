## **2\. What Keynetic does**

Keynetic provides a shared coordination platform for property moves.

The platform allows people involved in a property transaction to:

* create or join a property chain;  
* see relevant information about connected properties and participants;  
* receive updates about the progress of a move;  
* connect participants in the chain;  
* receive invitations to join a chain;  
* allow participating estate agents to coordinate properties and transactions; and  
* receive transactional communications relating to their account or property chain.

Keynetic is not a replacement for an estate agent's CRM or other professional systems. It provides a shared coordination layer between participants in a property chain.

# **3\. Information we collect**

The information we collect depends on how you use Keynetic.

### **Account information**

When you create an account, we may collect:

* your name;  
* email address;  
* account type;  
* authentication and account identifiers;  
* information required to maintain your account; and  
* records of your acceptance of relevant terms and policies.

### **Property and transaction information**

When you create or participate in a property move, we may process:

* property address information;  
* postcode;  
* property and chain identifiers;  
* your position within a property chain;  
* relationships between properties in a chain;  
* transaction or move status;  
* structured progress updates;  
* dates and timestamps associated with activity; and  
* information identifying participating users, estate agents, companies or branches.

Some information about other properties may be restricted depending on your position and participation in the chain.

Certain property information may therefore be redacted where a participant is not entitled to see it.

### **Estate-agent information**

Where an estate agent uses Keynetic, we may process:

* estate-agent contact details;  
* company and branch information;  
* staff and user account information;  
* property assignments;  
* chain participation;  
* subscription and billing information; and  
* records of activity performed through the platform.

### **Invitation information**

When someone invites another person to participate in a Keynetic property chain, we may process:

* the invited person's email address;  
* invitation name where supplied;  
* invitation status;  
* invitation expiry and related timestamps;  
* invitation identifiers and securely stored invitation token information; and  
* records relating to the invitation.

### **Communications information**

Keynetic sends transactional emails such as account, invitation and service-related notifications.

We may retain information about those communications, including:

* recipient email address;  
* message or template type;  
* delivery/provider identifiers;  
* timestamps;  
* delivery or processing status; and  
* limited error or provider information.

**Keynetic does not provide an arbitrary user-to-user messaging or chat facility.**

Users cannot use Keynetic's activity system to send unrestricted free-text messages to other participants. Progress information is based on structured updates and predefined options.

# **8\. Property chains and information about other participants**

A central feature of Keynetic is that property-chain participants need to see relevant information about the chain in order to coordinate a move.

If you join a property chain, information associated with that chain may therefore be visible to other participants who are entitled to access it.

Access is controlled according to participation in the chain and, where relevant, estate-agent assignment.

We do not provide unrestricted access to all information simply because a person has a Keynetic account.

Certain property information may be redacted depending on the participant's position and access rights.

### **9\. Estate agents and other organisations**

Estate agents and other organisations may use Keynetic as part of their own business activities. Those organisations remain responsible for their own processing of personal data for their own purposes.

Keynetic independently operates the shared property-chain platform and determines the purposes and means of processing required to provide, secure, maintain and improve that platform.

This includes operating property chains, managing access and visibility between authorised participants, maintaining platform security, managing the lifecycle of information held within the platform, and analysing information to improve Keynetic and develop platform-level insights.

An estate agent's use of Keynetic does not make Keynetic simply an extension of the estate agent's own systems or database. Keynetic operates as an independent platform.

Estate agents remain responsible for their own processing activities outside Keynetic, including information they collect and use for their own estate agency business.

A property chain may continue to operate even where an estate agent stops using Keynetic or its subscription ends. Where this occurs, Keynetic may continue to process information necessary to operate the shared chain for authorised participants, subject to the applicable lifecycle, retention and data protection requirements described in this Privacy Policy.

# **12\. How long we keep information**

We retain different categories of personal information for different periods depending on why we hold it, our operational requirements and applicable legal obligations.

We do not retain personal information indefinitely without a reason to do so.

Our detailed retention periods and the criteria used to determine them are set out in our **Data Retention Schedule**.

In general:

* active account information is retained while the account remains active;  
* property and chain information is retained for as long as it is operationally required;  
* certain email information is automatically minimised after defined periods;  
* expired invitation information is subject to retention and minimisation controls;  
* billing and commercial records may need to be retained for longer periods;  
* information needed for legal, security, fraud-prevention or regulatory purposes may be retained for the relevant period; and  
* genuinely anonymised information may be retained where it can no longer be linked to an identifiable person. Where information is used for platform analytics or modelling, we will seek to minimise the personal information included in analytical datasets and, where appropriate, use aggregated, anonymised or pseudonymised information.
